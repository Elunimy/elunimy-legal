Getting started

Elunimy is your personal knowledge base. Save a note, a link, a document, a photo, or a YouTube video in a single tap, and later find any of it by meaning - including by a word written inside a photo, buried in a PDF, or spoken in a video.

  • Save something: tap the add button and paste a link, add a photo, import a document, or write a note.
  • Find it later: use the search bar and type a word related to what you saved - you don't have to remember exactly what you called it, or which language you saved it in.

Your account & data

Your library is private to you. You can edit your name and manage your sign-in methods in the app's settings. For how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the app, under Settings. This permanently removes your library from our systems. If you'd prefer we do it for you, contact us from the email on your account.

Subscriptions

Elunimy is free to use, with an optional subscription for higher limits. Subscriptions are billed through your App Store or Google Play account, and you can manage or cancel them anytime in your device's subscription settings.

Report a problem

Found a bug, or something not working as expected? Contact us with a short description and, if you can, a screenshot. It helps us fix things faster.
