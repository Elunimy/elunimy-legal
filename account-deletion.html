Delete Your Account

Elunimy · Andrej Veremciuk

You can delete your Elunimy account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how, what gets removed, and how long it takes.

Delete from within the app

The fastest way is directly in the Elunimy app:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and its data are then permanently removed from our systems.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, you can ask us to delete your account for you. Email us from the address on your account:

Request deletion info@elunimy.com

Send from your account email so we can verify the request. We action deletion requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and profile (name, email, sign-in details).
  • Everything you saved - notes, links, documents, photos, and videos.
  • The files behind that content stored on our servers.
  • Your usage and analytics records tied to your account.

Timing and retention

In-app deletion is immediate and permanent. Items you had previously moved to the Bin are held for up to 30 days before permanent removal, and any residual copies in routine backups are purged within a reasonable period as part of our normal backup cycle. We do not keep your content after deletion, except where the law requires us to retain limited records.

For more on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
